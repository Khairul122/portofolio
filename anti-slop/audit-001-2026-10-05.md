## Checked and left as is

- Copy: no em dashes in the UI text, no buzzwords from R-16, no generic CTAs, no emoji, no invented
  statistics or testimonials. Every number comes from the GitHub data, a labelled count, or the
  owner's own CV.
- Hero character cards: the nicknames ("THE SHIPPER" and similar) are a deliberate voice taken from the
  owner's reference image, not a statistic or a claim, so they stay.
- Tech Stack bubbles, Journey timeline, Activity heatmap, Lanyard and Intro Video are specific to this
  owner's data or interaction and are not templates.
- Identity motif (cut corners, skewed bold titles, red accent) is the declared direction in DESIGN.md.
  It is repeated on purpose.
- The Journey cards still alternate left and right with the same card shape. Content is real and the
  alternation tracks the timeline, so this is a candidate for a later pass, not a defect.
